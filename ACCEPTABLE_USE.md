# Acceptable Use

`ezdxf_mcp` may be used only for lawful, authorized, noncommercial purposes
permitted by the project license.

## Required conduct

- Obtain permission to access and automate the target system.
- Follow applicable law, contracts, privacy obligations, and platform rules.
- Use reasonable pacing and data minimization.
- Protect credentials, session data, screenshots, extracted data, and DXF
  artifacts.
- Stop or request human intervention when a service presents a CAPTCHA,
  authentication request, denial, rate limit, consent decision, or other
  legitimate control.
- Keep a human approval step for destructive, financial, account-changing, or
  otherwise consequential actions.

## Prohibited conduct

- CAPTCHA, authentication, paywall, or access-control bypass.
- Anti-bot evasion, identity deception, fingerprint spoofing, or session
  hijacking.
- Credential theft, phishing, malware, exploitation, or unauthorized access.
- Collection of personal, confidential, or protected data without a lawful
  basis and permission.
- High-rate or disruptive traffic, denial of service, or attempts to avoid
  published limits.
- Commercial use without a separate written commercial license.

Visible, human-paced interaction can reduce abnormal automation patterns, but
it does not guarantee that a third-party service will allow automation or that
an account will never be restricted. Operators remain responsible for every
target, action, and collected record.
